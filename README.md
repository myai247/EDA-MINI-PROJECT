Dataset Introduction:

This analysis utilizes the Health & Lifestyle Dataset (sourced from Kaggle: emmanualikku/health-lifestyle-dataset-synthetic), which captures physical, clinical, and behavioral indicators across 200 individual patients. The objective of this dataset is to investigate how lifestyle choices such as daily physical activity, sleep duration, exercise frequency, and smoking interact with physiological markers like BMI, blood pressure, and cholesterol to determine overall health risk.
•	Dataset Dimensions: 200 rows × 13 columns (prior to cleaning).
•	Target Domain: Preventive healthcare, lifestyle analysis, and health risk stratifications.

The analysis demonstrates that clinical markers (Blood Pressure) and high-risk behaviors (Smoking Status) are the primary drivers of health risk classification. 100% of active smokers and 100% of patients with High Blood Pressure fall into the High Health Risk category. BMI acts as a key continuous marker, with high-risk patients averaging 29.52 kg/m² compared to 21.54 kg/m² for low-risk individuals.
